# PROFITX AI project guidance

## Brand continuity

Before changing branding, colors, logos or interface styling, read `docs/PFX_BRAND_REFERENCE.md` and `docs/PFX_OBSERVATORY_DESIGN.md`. The user's uploaded reference is authoritative: deep black, metallic silver, electric cyan/blue, saturated violet/magenta, neon PFX green. Preserve the official logo and do not substitute a muted pastel interpretation. Keep risk/status colors meaningful and mobile/accessibility behavior intact.
